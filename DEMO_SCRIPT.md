# JumpStart — Loom Demo Script (~90 sec)

**Audience:** general / potential users
**Goal:** show the pain it solves, then the two moments that sell it: one-click control + the AI Kanban.
**Format:** each scene has an on-screen action, an on-screen text caption, and the words to say. Target ~215 spoken words.

---

### Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:12)
**On screen:** Cluttered desktop with terminal tabs, then open JumpStart.
**On-screen text:** `Too many terminals?`

> "If you juggle more than one project, your day is a dozen terminal tabs, half of them still running. This is JumpStart, one control panel for every app and project on your machine."

---

### Scene 2 — The library (0:12–0:27)
**On screen:** Project library with recent / most-used shortcuts.
**On-screen text:** `Add once → auto-detected`

> "Add a project once and JumpStart detects what can run: Node, Go, Python, Docker, even monorepos. Recent and most-used are pinned right at the top."

---

### Scene 3 — One-click start/stop (0:27–0:57)
**On screen:** Click Start. Show live status, CPU/memory, port badge appearing. Click the localhost port badge.
**On-screen text:** `One click. Live status + clickable ports.`

> "One click starts a process, or the whole project. You get live status, CPU, and memory, and JumpStart pulls the port out of the logs, so this badge jumps straight to localhost. Logs stream live, so you always know where something broke."

---

### Scene 4 — The AI Kanban board (0:57–1:22)
**On screen:** Open board, click a card, hit "Fill with AI," then the story-assistant chat.
**On-screen text:** `Every project gets an AI board`

> "Every project also gets its own board. Click any card and hit 'Fill with AI' to draft the description, acceptance criteria, and subtasks with a local model. There's even a chat that spins up whole batches of stories onto the board."

---

### Scene 5 — Close (1:22–1:30)
**On screen:** Zoom out to full app, native macOS look.
**On-screen text:** `JumpStart — everything in one window`

> "One window, native feel, no more terminal hunting. That's JumpStart. Link's below."

---

## Quick tips for recording
- Stage the messy-desktop shot before you hit record so the hook lands.
- Move the cursor deliberately; pause half a second before each click.
- Have one project pre-configured with a real port so the badge demo works live.
- Pre-load the Ollama model so "Fill with AI" responds fast.
- Keep captions short (3–5 words) and on screen ~3 seconds; place them top-center so they don't cover UI.
